1. Introduction

Welcome to yt.reelssaver ("we", "our", "us", or "ReelsSaver"). We operate the free media conversion and downloading tool accessible at our web domain. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to maintaining transparent data management standards.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use our service, how URL data is handled, and how your privacy rights are safeguarded under global privacy frameworks including GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many online download services, yt.reelssaver is built on a Zero-Tracking architecture:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or payment details.
  • No User Account: You can download videos, Reels, and audio anonymously without ever signing up or logging in.
  • No Download History or Logfiles: We do not log which videos you convert or download, nor do we associate media URLs with your IP address.
  • No Device Fingerprinting: We do not deploy fingerprinting scripts or intrusive device analytics.

3. How Video URLs and Media Processing Work

When you submit a URL from YouTube, Instagram Reels, or TikTok into our search bar:

  1. The submitted URL is parsed in real-time by open-source extraction utilities (such as yt-dlp) to retrieve publicly accessible metadata (title, available resolutions, bitrate).
  2. When you select a download quality (e.g. 1080p MP4 or 320kbps MP3), the media stream is packaged for your direct download.
  3. Media files downloaded to your device are temporarily cached locally on your device or host system strictly for completion and are not indexed or publicly displayed.

4. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

We do not use tracking cookies, cross-site trackers, or third-party behavioral advertising cookies.

You can clear or delete your local storage data at any time via your browser settings.
